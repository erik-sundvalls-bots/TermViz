TermViz CDR KG explorer: prototypes

Design prototypes for showing openEHR composition instances, templates and archetypes under the terminology pane of the TermViz split view. V7 is the target design, to be built piece by piece in TermViz.

Overview

  • All variants comparedwrite-ups, comparison table, recommendation, sources

Versions

  • V1 Three stacked bands (static mockup)
    Three stacked bands: ontology DAG on top, template and archetype trees in the middle, patient instances at the bottom.
  • V2 Template icicle with instance heat (static mockup)
    The whole IPS template as an icicle, coloured by how much the example data uses each part.
  • V3 Archetype-centric hub (static mockup)
    Archetypes as the hub, packed circles showing reuse across templates and patients.
  • V4 Patient timeline of composition tiles (static mockup)
    Each patient's record as a timeline of composition tiles.
  • V5 Zoomable nested treemap + ontology icicle, bundled edges (interactive demo)
    Zoomable nested treemap of patients and an ontology icicle, with bundled edges.
  • V6 Three-layer stack: ontology DAG, archetypes over templates, cascaded treemaps (interactive demo)
    Three-layer stack: locked Sugiyama DAG, archetypes over templates, cascaded treemaps of instances.
  • V7 Collapsible stack: clustered DAG, openable archetypes and templates, shrinkable instances (interactive demo) target design · English version (all labels in English)
    Target design. V6 plus collapse and expand in every layer, clustered SNOMED CT DAG with +n pills, and links that are bundled with counts or split into dotted element-level links.
